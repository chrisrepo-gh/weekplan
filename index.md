# Weekly Plan: KIDS Week (July 27 - August 2, 2026)

## Monday, July 27
* **Playmobil FunPark (Zirndorf)**
  * Time: 09:00 - 19:00
  * Location: Brandstätterstraße 2-10, Zirndorf
  * Why: Ideal for ages 8-11, interactive outdoor play areas and climbing structures.
  * Link: https://www.playmobil-funpark.de
  * Registration Required: Yes (Ticket)

## Tuesday, July 28
* **Nuremberg Zoo (Tiergarten Nürnberg)**
  * Time: 08:00 - 19:30
  * Location: Am Tiergarten 30, Nuremberg
  * Why: Large grounds, dolphin lagoon, and manatee house are hits for pre-teens.
  * Link: https://tiergarten.nuernberg.de
  * Registration Required: No

## Wednesday, July 29
* **Museum for Communication (Museum für Kommunikation Nürnberg)**
  * Time: 09:00 - 17:00
  * Location: Lessingstraße 6, Nuremberg
  * Why: Interactive exhibits on technology and postal history, highly engaging for tech-savvy boys.
  * Link: https://www.mfk-nuernberg.de
  * Registration Required: No

## Thursday, July 30
* **Boulder World Erlangen**
  * Time: 10:00 - 23:00
  * Location: Güterbahnhofstraße 3, Erlangen
  * Why: Excellent physical activity; separate bouldering areas suitable for different skill levels (8 and 11 years old).
  * Link: https://boulderwelt-erlangen.de
  * Registration Required: No

## Friday, July 31
* **Franken-Therme Bad Windsheim (Outdoor Pool Area)**
  * Time: 09:00 - 22:00
  * Location: Erkenbrechtallee 10, Bad Windsheim
  * Why: Great summer getaway with salt lakes and expansive lawns for active boys.
  * Link: https://www.franken-therme.net
  * Registration Required: No

## Saturday, August 1
* **DB Museum (German Railway Museum)**
  * Time: 09:00 - 18:00
  * Location: Lessingstraße 6, Nuremberg
  * Why: Large-scale train displays and driving simulators are very popular with boys in this age group.
  * Link: https://dbmuseum.de
  * Registration Required: No

## Sunday, August 2
* **Erlangen Schlossgarten and Botanical Garden**
  * Time: 08:00 - 20:00
  * Location: Schlossgarten, Erlangen
  * Why: Open space for running, frisbee, or a relaxed picnic to end the week.
  * Link: https://www.erlangen.de
  * Registration Required: No
