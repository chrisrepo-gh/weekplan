# Weekly Activity Plan: KIDS Week
**Date Range:** 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-27

## Activities for Boys (Ages 8 & 11)

*   **Tiergarten Nürnberg (Nuremberg Zoo)**
    *   **Date/Time:** 2026-09-21 (Anytime 09:00 - 19:30)
    *   **Location:** Am Tiergarten 30, 90480 Nürnberg
    *   **Why:** Perfect for boys to explore the dolphin lagoon and manatee house. High engagement for all ages.
    *   **Link:** [https://tiergarten.nuernberg.de](https://tiergarten.nuernberg.de)
    *   **Tickets Required:** Yes, recommend online booking.

*   **Franken-Therme Bad Windsheim (Saltwater Pool)**
    *   **Date/Time:** 2026-09-22 (10:00 - 22:00)
    *   **Location:** Erkenbrechtallee 10, 91438 Bad Windsheim
    *   **Why:** Excellent indoor and outdoor pools for active kids to burn energy.
    *   **Link:** [https://www.franken-therme.net](https://www.franken-therme.net)
    *   **Tickets Required:** No.

*   **Schloss Thurn Family Park**
    *   **Date/Time:** 2026-09-23 (10:00 - 17:00)
    *   **Location:** Schlossplatz 4, 91336 Heroldsbach
    *   **Why:** Features various rides and shows specifically tailored for ages 8-12.
    *   **Link:** [https://www.schloss-thurn.de](https://www.schloss-thurn.de)
    *   **Tickets Required:** Yes.

*   **Deutsches Museum Nürnberg (Future Museum)**
    *   **Date/Time:** 2026-09-24 (10:00 - 18:00)
    *   **Location:** Augustinerhof, 90403 Nürnberg
    *   **Why:** Highly interactive exhibits on robots, space, and the future perfectly suited for curious minds.
    *   **Link:** [https://www.deutsches-museum.de/nuernberg](https://www.deutsches-museum.de/nuernberg)
    *   **Tickets Required:** Yes, recommended.

*   **Boulderhalle Café Kraft**
    *   **Date/Time:** 2026-09-25 (10:00 - 23:00)
    *   **Location:** Gebertstraße 2, 90411 Nürnberg
    *   **Why:** World-class bouldering facility where the kids can safely practice climbing.
    *   **Link:** [https://cafekraft.de](https://cafekraft.de)
    *   **Tickets Required:** No.

*   **Playmobil FunPark**
    *   **Date/Time:** 2026-09-26 (09:00 - 19:00)
    *   **Location:** Brandstätterstraße 2-10, 90513 Zirndorf
    *   **Why:** The best interactive playground in the region with massive structures for active play.
    *   **Link:** [https://www.playmobil-funpark.de](https://www.playmobil-funpark.de)
    *   **Tickets Required:** Yes, online only.

*   **Erlangen City Museum (Stadtmuseum)**
    *   **Date/Time:** 2026-09-27 (11:00 - 17:00)
    *   **Location:** Hauptstraße 80, 91054 Erlangen
    *   **Why:** Educational look at the history of Erlangen; engaging for local context.
    *   **Link:** [https://stadtmuseum-erlangen.de](https://stadtmuseum-erlangen.de)
    *   **Tickets Required:** No.
